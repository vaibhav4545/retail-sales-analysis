# Key Business Insights (dashboard text)

All figures are checked against the SQL results.

**01 | SEASONALITY AND WEEKENDS:** October is the peak month (₹48.48 Cr, about 14% of the year) and has the highest average bill (₹7,120), while February is the weakest (₹15.21 Cr). Weekends earn about 1.7 times more per day than weekdays (average ₹131 lakh vs ₹78 lakh). Plan stock and staff for festive weekends.

**02 | SOUTH CONCENTRATION:** South brings about 67% of revenue (₹228.37 Cr) from 139 of 258 stores. Each South store earns about ₹1.64 Cr, against about ₹0.73 Cr in North, so it leads on both store count and store performance. Check what North and East stores can copy from top South stores.

**03 | ONLINE: SMALL BILLS, HEAVY DISCOUNTS:** The online store (ROOT) has the smallest average bill (₹2,527 vs ₹6,889 in South) and the highest discount rate (14.57% vs 1.8 to 2.8% in stores). It is worth testing whether online sales depend on discounts.

**Limitations:** no cost or profit data, so margins are not analysed. 2022 data only.
